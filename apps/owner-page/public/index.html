How it works

A small background service runs on your own machine, separate from any AI.

Capture
It records what your agent is working on as you go. There is no save button, and it still works if the agent is killed.
Encrypt
It compiles that work into a checkpoint, encrypts it with keys on your machine, and records it on the Monad blockchain, on testnet today.
Share
You decide which AI can read or write which kinds of context. Approvals, revocations and who wrote each record are all kept on chain.

For teams building with AI

Building an AI app or an agent harness? Integrate Mida through the SDK and the MCP server (the standard way agents plug into tools). Your users arrive with context instead of as strangers, and you skip building memory, encryption, permissions and provenance yourself.

  • The user holds the authority

    Permissions belong to the person, not to each app. Your app asks. They approve or revoke.

  • Provenance on chain

    Every record shows which agent wrote it.

  • Keys stay with the user

    Context is encrypted on their machine. You never hold their keys.
